# Recording guide: Tunisian voiceover

Record the 9 lines below **in order**, in one file. **Leave about 1 second of silence between lines.**
I use those pauses to find where each line starts and sync the whole video to your voice.

1. تخيّل روحك تمشي للـ supermarché في بلاد أخرى، تلقى منتوج يعجبك، أما المكوّنات مكتوبين بلغة ما تفهمهاش.
2. و حتى كان تفهمها، ساعات ما تعرفش شنوّا معناها.
3. And that's why I built Novi AI.
4. An application powered by AI,
5. باش تعاونك تفهم شنيّا المنتوجات إلّي تشري: تصوّر la liste des ingrédients، و هو يترجمهم على حسب احتياجاتك.
6. سوى عندك حساسية من paraben، sulfate ولا silicone،
7. ولا تحب تلقى بدائل لمنتوجك المفضّل في بلاد أخرى.
8. و أحسن حاجة، it supports 12 languages.
9. خاطر موش المهم كان شنوّا تشري، المهم زادة تعرف شنوّا فيه.

**Tips**
- Use a quiet room with soft furniture (a bedroom or closet works well), not a bathroom or kitchen.
- Hold the phone about 15–20 cm from your mouth, slightly to the side so your breath doesn't hit the mic.
- Smile while you talk; it makes the voice sound warmer. Keep your energy up, like you're telling a friend.
- If you make a mistake, pause and re-read the whole line, then tell me which take to keep (or just send the clean version).
- Pause briefly at the commas in lines 1 and 9; the on-screen text switches there.
- Send the original file (Voice Memos .m4a, .mp3 or .wav are all fine). Please don't send it through WhatsApp, which compresses audio.
