# Novi style guide

Rules every edit in this studio follows. Reference reels for edit style and captions live in
`media/reference/` (see "Reference reels" below).

## Colour

- **Captions are pink.** That is the brand colour: `--brand-pink: #FF6FA8`
  (change the hex here if you have an exact one, and every edit follows it).
- **No purple and no neon**: no violet/magenta-blue tints, no glowing neon edges or neon light looks,
  in captions, graphics, grades or backgrounds.
- Supporting colours stay neutral: white `#FFFFFF` and near-black `#111111` for caption outline or
  shadow; any other accent comes from the footage, not a new colour.

## Captions

- Fill: `--brand-pink`. Outline or shadow in near-black so they read on any footage; no glow.
- Word timings come from `scripts/transcribe.py` (`*.words.json`).
- Stay inside the 80% title-safe area; for 9:16 keep clear of the bottom ~20% (app UI).
